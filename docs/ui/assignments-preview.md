# Assignments and submissions preview

The teacher, student, parent, and administrator dashboards now include `/teacher/assignments`, `/student/assignments`, `/parent/assignments`, and `/admin/assignments` respectively. Shared typed contracts, state transitions, validation, and UI live in `src/features/assignments`.

Teachers publish a title, instructions, active assigned group, deadline date, and maximum score. A managed, non-cancelled lesson from the same group can be linked optionally. Publication requires at least one active student and snapshots the recipients, group name, teacher name, and linked lesson title. Later group changes do not change the recipient list. Cancelling a linked lesson does not cancel the assignment.

Students submit a text answer and may replace it until grading. The latest answer and submission time replace the previous ungraded answer. The deadline runs through the end of the chosen day in Africa/Cairo; later submissions are accepted and labelled late. No automatic penalty or zero grade is applied. These are explicit preview behaviors, not approved production assessment policies.

Teachers grade submitted answers from zero to the assignment maximum and provide feedback. Fractional scores are allowed; the maximum is an integer from 1 to 1000. Grades and feedback can be corrected by the teacher. A graded answer cannot be replaced by the student. Missing answers remain ungraded. Parents see only a selected currently linked child's answer and result. Administrators have read-only review with teacher, group, and submission-status filters. For staff, a status filter matches an assignment if at least one recipient has that status; late submission remains noted after grading.

Publishing and submitting require the respective active preview account. Existing records remain readable as history. Fixed sample identities and frontend visibility checks are not real authorization. State is held under the portal layout, shared across role previews, and resets on reload or leaving that layout. No storage, backend, Supabase, real notification, file upload, or payment integration was added.

Limitations: published assignments cannot yet be edited, withdrawn, or reopened; attachments and version history are not implemented. The older illustrative teacher review table remains separate from this new assignment workflow. Production publication rules, late penalties, appeals, and retention require business review before backend integration.

Validation: production build and TypeScript checks passed. ESLint reported zero errors and the existing `next/no-img-element` logo warning in `src/components/shared.tsx`. All 104 Playwright tests passed, including eight new assignment tests covering Cairo deadlines, late state, zero grades, submission replacement and locking, guardian scoping, roster snapshots, empty groups, lesson linkage, reset behavior, absence of external writes, accessibility, and responsive layouts at 375/768/1440px. Browser error monitoring passed for the complete submission and grading journey. Screenshots: `previews/teacher-assignments-desktop.png` and `previews/student-assignments-mobile.png`. Native screen reader and device testing were not performed.
